
A Duel

Scene 1: normal conversation

Scene 2: a seated argument starts as casually as possible about something seemingly mundane

Scene 3: as they've been walking and talking we find ourselves watching them still arguing in good humor as they enter a stadium

Scene 4: the argument gets heated the moment they step onto the grass

Scene 5: somehow they get off the grass and into a fantasy world filled mostly with marble and crystalized water features.

Scene 6: they are now as heated as we care to make them. (violence, insults, etc)?

Scene 7: they reach what looks like a formal game show podium, shake hands firmly, about face, and march off back in the direction they came, picking back up where they were in Scene 1 without a hitch.
