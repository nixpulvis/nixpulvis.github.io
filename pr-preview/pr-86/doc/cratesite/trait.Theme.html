Expand description

What a site makes of its pages: its templates and layouts, and any files of its own. A site implements it on a type of its own holding its Site, and anything else its templates need, and builds with Theme::build.

fn template(&self, page: &Page<Self::Extra>) -> Option<Result<String, String>>

The page’s Markdown with its template tags rendered, for a page with them, front matter and all. The build script makes an askama template for each such page, so this is usually its generated render.
