I’ve been working on Galos, on and off, for almost six years now. This is the story of how a script for tracking a faction turned into a map of the galaxy.

2021: The Faction

The first commit is from February 23rd, 2021. It just printed the EDDN feed to the terminal. Within a few days it was saving systems and factions to Postgres, and by the end of the month it was tracking influence.

2024: A Map

In August 2024 it came back with a burst of work. Importing from the EDSM API, stations with their services and economies, and then the start of a 3D map in Bevy. By the end of September I could fly a camera around, search for a system, and see a route drawn through the stars.
